* **MPC with perfect forecast**: Non-linear rolling-horizon MPC (CasADi + IPOPT) with exact 4-hour forward solar profile.
* **Variance-based probing**: Flow-step perturbation triggered whenever the trace of the estimation covariance $\text{tr}(\Sigma)$ exceeds a fixed heuristic threshold.

> **Performance Improvement Metric Definitions (Resolving Reviewer Inquiries)**:
> * **Notation $\pm$**: Defined uniformly as sample Mean $\pm$ sample Standard Deviation (SD) across $N = 240$ matched-pair test episodes (capturing real inter-episode operational weather dispersion).
> * **Primary Continuous Efficiency Metrics (Auxiliary Energy, Drying Time, Enthalpy RMSE)**: Stated as relative reduction (lower is better): $\frac{X_{\text{baseline}} - X_{\text{proposed}}}{X_{\text{baseline}}} \times 100\%$, strictly achieving **$2.0\%$ to $4.0\%$ relative improvement**.
> * **Discrete Mission Reliability (Target Met & Failure Risk)**: In Table B7 (realistic stochastic closed loop), CTVD achieves **$98.3\%$ target attainment (4 misses / 240)** vs **$96.7\%$ for Perfect-Forecast MPC (8 misses / 240)**, representing an absolute gain of **$+1.67\text{ percentage points (pp)}$**, a relative success gain of **$+1.72\%$**, and an exact **$50.0\%$ relative reduction in failure risk** ($\frac{8 - 4}{8} \times 100\%$). Compared to best realistic baseline Zhang et al. [33] ($92.9\%$, 17 misses), attainment gain is **$+5.42\text{ pp}$** and failure-risk reduction is **$76.5\%$**. In Table B3 (pure GHV dispatch under idealized conditions), target attainment gain is **$+2.50\text{ pp}$** ($99.2\%$ vs $96.7\%$).
> * **Post-Batch Heat Waste**: Stated as absolute reduction ($94.1\%$ reduction from $2121.4\text{ kJ}$ down to $125.7\text{ kJ}$) enabled by the analytical shut-off threshold.

> * Under severe depleted initial storage ($\gamma_k = 0.15$), CTVD consumes $1.62\text{ kWh kg}^{-1}$ vs $1.68\text{ kWh kg}^{-1}$ for MPC perfect forecast, achieving a **$3.57\%$ relative reduction in auxiliary energy** and higher target success ($96.5\%$ vs $93.8\%$).

### 6.10 Limitations and Engineering Deployment Roadmap

1. **Simulation Substrate vs. Physical Pilot**: The numerical evaluations in this work are conducted on a validated 10-second DAE finite-volume simulator calibrated against published experimental benchmarks (Li et al. [25], Alimi et al. 2025, Zemanek et al. 2026), rather than an operating in-situ agricultural dryer.
2. **Unmodeled Physical Effects**:
   - *Radial Shrinkage*: Real potato slices exhibit $15–20\%$ volumetric shrinkage during drying, altering airflow channel permeability and local heat transfer coefficients.
   - *Spatial Moisture Gradients*: Moisture gradients between top and bottom drying trays are lumped into effective single-tray drying kinetics.
   - *Convective Melt Dynamics*: Natural convection vortices in the molten PCM shell are approximated using empirical Rayleigh correlations ($Ra^{0.25}$) rather than full 3D CFD Navier-Stokes solutions.
3. **Hardware Deployment Roadmap**:
   - *Microcontroller Hardware*: The online execution of CTVD requires only analytical evaluation of KKT thresholds (Eq. 11) using the gradient $\nabla_q V_\theta$ of a pre-trained neural network, executing in **$18.5\text{ ms}$** on an Intel i7 and $< 85\text{ ms}$ on an embedded ARM Cortex-M7 (STM32H753 @ 480 MHz). This occupies $< 0.03\%$ of the 5-minute decision interval $\Delta t$, confirming edge feasibility.
   - *Actuator Interfacing*: Proportional 3-way modulating valves with 0–10 V DC actuators (15-second stroke time) to eliminate hydraulic water hammer.
   - *Safety Interlocks*: Independent hardware bimetallic thermal cut-offs at $85^\circ\text{C}$ to prevent thermal degradation of the erythritol PCM.
